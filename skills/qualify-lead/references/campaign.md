# Demonstration campaign

Synthetic campaign: budget at least BRL 300,000 and timeline at most 90 days. Zero means unknown. Consent must be explicit true before drafting outreach. Missing consent produces no_contact; missing budget or timeline produces needs_information; matching complete records are sales_ready, otherwise nurture. Email comparison is case-insensitive.

These are demonstration assumptions, not investment advice or housing/credit eligibility. Changes must be reflected in core.qualify and tests; Markdown alone does not change execution.
